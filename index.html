A modular scientific toolkit

The project is designed to keep equations, numerical assumptions, extracted arrays, and visual diagnostics easy to inspect.

Inflationary dynamics

Background evolution, single-field models, axion–gauge systems, and ultra-slow-roll transitions.

Perturbations

Linear modes, tensor production, Floquet diagnostics, and transparent spectrum calculations.

Early-Universe signals

Primordial black holes, magnetogenesis, gravitational waves, and related cosmological observables.

Learn by reproducing

Julia and Python notebooks will accompany the code, with Mathematica scripts for analytic checks and theory-first derivations.

For students

Small, explicit examples that connect equations to numerical solutions and plots.

For researchers

Benchmark reproductions, documented conventions, and reusable analysis workflows.

For contributors

A living codebase that can grow through focused modules and community feedback.

Development status

InflationLab is being developed alongside current research. The Julia solver and notebooks will be released with a tagged, documented version of the associated work.
